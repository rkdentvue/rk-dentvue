Services

Complete dental radiology under one roof.

From single-tooth detail to full-arch 3D volumes, we offer the full spectrum of dental imaging.

OPG (Panoramic)

Full-arch panoramic X-ray capturing both jaws in a single scan — ideal for screening, extractions and general assessment.

CBCT (3D Imaging)

High-resolution cone-beam CT for implant planning, impacted teeth, pathology and complex endodontic cases.

Cephalometry

Lateral cephalometric radiographs for orthodontic diagnosis, growth analysis and treatment planning.

Intraoral X-rays

Periapical and bitewing radiographs for detailed tooth-level diagnosis and caries detection.

Implant Planning

CBCT-based 3D assessment with measurements and nerve mapping for safe implant placement.

Digital Reports

Fast, secure digital delivery of scans and reports to referring dentists and patients.
