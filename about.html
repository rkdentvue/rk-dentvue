About

Imaging that puts your smile first.

RK Dentvue Centres is a dedicated dental radiology network delivering high-resolution OPG, CBCT and cephalometric scans to dentists and patients across Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.

Who we are

Our centres combine modern imaging equipment with experienced radiology teams to produce clear, accurate reports — fast.

From routine check-ups to complex implant and orthodontic planning, we provide the diagnostic clarity clinicians rely on. We are a diagnostic imaging network — not a dental clinic — supporting dentists with the scans they need for confident treatment planning.

  • High-resolution 3D CBCT and panoramic imaging
  • Digital reports and DICOM files in 24-48 hrs
  • Patient-first, low-dose protocols
  • Three convenient locations across two states

Led by

P. Ravindra Reddy — Director, RK Dentvue, with a focus on precision diagnostics, patient safety and close collaboration with referring dentists.

We work closely with referring dentists and share digital reports promptly.
